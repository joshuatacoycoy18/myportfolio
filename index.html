04. How We'd Work Together

A simple, transparent process, so you know what happens after you send a message.

  1. Initial discussionYou share your goals, examples you like, and any deadline.
  2. Requirements & scopeWe agree on deliverables, formats or pages, and what's out of scope.
  3. Proposal & quoteYou get a written quote and timeline before any work starts.
  4. Editing or developmentI build or edit, and share progress at agreed checkpoints.
  5. Review & revisionsYou review and request changes. Revision rounds are set in the proposal.
  6. DeliveryFinal files or a launched site, plus any handover notes you need.
